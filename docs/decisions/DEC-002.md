# DEC-002 — Shape of Freshness Configuration for REQ-CLI-006

## Question
Where is the staleness threshold declared, and is it one value or per record type?

## Why it is open
`REQ-CLI-006` is the last M6 blocker and cannot be implemented until freshness is configurable. Stage gate enforcement depends on the same mechanism, so this decision unblocks two things.

## Options
1. **Single threshold in the manifest.** `freshness: max_age_days: 30`. Simple, one value, easy to reason about. Cost: a STATUS record and an ADR age at very different rates; one number will be wrong for one of them.
2. **Per record type.** `freshness: STATUS: 7, REQ: 90, ADR: 0`. Accurate. Cost: more manifest surface, and the narrow parser grows.
3. **Per record override in frontmatter.** Each record declares its own tolerance. Most accurate, most scattered, and easiest to abuse by setting a long tolerance on the record that most needs watching.

## Blocks
M6 completion, and stage gate enforcement behind it.

## Resolution
Unresolved.
